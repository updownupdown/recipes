MyRecipes privacy

MyRecipes is a personal recipe app. It has no server and collects no data.

  • Your recipes are stored in your browser on your own device.
  • If you connect Google Drive, the app uses the drive.file permission to save a MyRecipes/recipes.json file (and any Google Docs you export) in your own Drive. It can only see files it created. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • Your Google sign-in token is kept only in your browser and is used only to talk to Google Drive.
  • Shared recipe links contain the recipe itself in the link; it isn't stored on any server.

To remove access, disconnect in the app, or remove MyRecipes at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
