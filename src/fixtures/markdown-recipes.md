## Garlic Greens With Lemon

**Ingredients**

* 1 pound greens, trimmed and chopped
* Extra-virgin olive oil
* 3 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
* 1/4 teaspoon dried chile flakes
* Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
* Juice of 1 lemon

**Steps**

1. Heat a glug of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat and add the garlic. Cook until golden, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the chile flakes and the greens, season with salt and pepper, and toss to coat.
3. Cook until the greens are wilted and tender, 6 to 8 minutes.
4. Squeeze over the lemon juice, toss again, and taste for seasoning.
5. Serve hot or at room temperature.



Notes: Serves 4. Any sturdy greens work here, such as kale or chard.

## Roasted "Crispy" Carrots With Yogurt, Honey, And Seeds

**Ingredients**

* 2 pounds carrots, halved lengthwise
* Extra-virgin olive oil
* Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
* 1/2 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
* 1 tablespoon honey
* 2 tablespoons sesame seeds, toasted

**Steps**

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the carrots with olive oil, 1 teaspoon salt, and pepper.
3. Roast on a rimmed baking sheet until browned and tender, 25 to 30 minutes.
4. Stir the honey into the yogurt and spread it on a platter.
5. Pile the carrots on top and scatter over the sesame seeds.



Notes: Serves 4 to 6. The yogurt can be made a day ahead.

## Quick Tomato Pasta

**Ingredients**

* Kosher salt
* 12 ounces spaghetti
* 1/4 cup olive oil
* 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
* One 14-ounce can whole peeled tomatoes
* 1/2 cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese

**Steps**

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it well.
2. Warm the oil and garlic in a skillet over medium heat until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
3. Crush in the tomatoes and simmer until thick, 10 to 12 minutes.
4. Cook the pasta until 2 minutes shy of al dente, then drain, saving 1 cup of the water.
5. Toss the pasta with the sauce and cheese, loosening with pasta water. Serve right away.



Notes: Serves 4.
